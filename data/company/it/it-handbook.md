# IT Handbook

## 1. Passwords

To reset your password, open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". The reset link expires after 30 minutes. Passwords must have at least 12 characters.

## 2. Help desk

The IT help desk is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00, on extension 4400 or helpdesk@example.com.

## 3. Laptops

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so they can be locked remotely.

## 4. VPN

Use the VPN whenever you access internal systems from outside the office.
