# Security Incident Response Plan

## 1. Classification

Incidents are classified as SEV-1 (customer data exposed), SEV-2 (internal systems compromised) or SEV-3 (suspicious activity).

## 2. First hour

For a SEV-1 incident, the incident commander opens the war room under the codename BLUEJAY, isolates the affected systems and informs the legal team within one hour.

## 3. Communication

Only the communications lead may speak to customers or the press about an incident.
