# Annual Leave Policy (2024)

## 1. Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

## 2. Carry-over

Up to 3 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 January.

## 3. Requests

Leave requests are sent by email to your manager at least one week in advance.
