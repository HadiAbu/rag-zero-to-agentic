# Annual Leave Policy (2026)

## 1. Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rata amount.

## 2. Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Days not used by then are lost.

## 3. Requests

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Managers approve or decline within three working days.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of absence.
