# Remote Work Policy

## 1. How often

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00.

## 2. Working abroad

Working from another country for more than 10 days per year needs HR approval because of tax and insurance rules.
