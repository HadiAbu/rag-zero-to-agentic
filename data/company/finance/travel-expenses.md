# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Submitting claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date in the finance portal. Attach an itemised receipt for every claim above 25 euros. Approved claims are paid within 10 business days.

## 2. Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours when a director approves it in advance.

## 3. Hotels

The hotel limit is 150 euros per night in major cities and 110 euros per night elsewhere.

## 4. Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 45 euros. Client dinners must not exceed 80 euros per person.
