# Team Work Policy (POL-204)

## 1. Shared responsibility

All members of a team share responsibility for the submission. If one team member submits late, the whole team's submission is marked late and the late penalty applies to every member.

## 2. Peer review

Each member completes a confidential peer review at the end of the project. A member who did not contribute can receive a lower individual grade than the rest of the team.

## 3. Disputes

Teams that cannot resolve a conflict should contact the course coordinator before the deadline, not after it.
