# Course FAQ

## 1. Grading

The final grade is made up of weekly labs (30%), a quiz (30%) and the capstone project (40%).

## 2. Labs

Labs are released every Monday and are due the following Sunday at 23:59. The lowest lab score is dropped.

## 3. Tools

You may use any programming language, but the course materials and examples use Python 3.10 or newer. Free local models are fine for all labs.

## 4. Attendance

Attendance is recorded in every live session. Students need at least 80% attendance to sit the final quiz.

## 5. Office hours

Course office hours are on Tuesdays from 14:00 to 15:00 in room B12, or online by appointment.
