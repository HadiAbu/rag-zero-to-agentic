# Team Assignment Brief

## 1. Identifiers

Team assignment project ID: A17. Use A17 in every file name and in the subject line of every email about the assignment. Policy POL-204 covers how team submissions are handled.

## 2. Teams

Teams have 3 or 4 members. Each team chooses one member as the submitter, who uploads the final files for everyone.

## 3. Deliverables

Each team delivers a working RAG prototype, an evaluation scoreboard comparing at least three retrieval configurations, and a short design review presented in class.
